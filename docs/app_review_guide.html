1. Sign-In & Authentication

✓ Native "Sign in with Apple" Enabled
Trimm does not require a legacy username and password. Reviewers can sign in seamlessly with one tap using Sign in with Apple directly on any Apple review device or simulator.
  • Sign-in method: Tap the native white "Sign in with Apple" button on the welcome screen.
  • Automatic review balance: Every newly authenticated account is automatically granted 20 free starter credits upon first sign-in, which provides ample balance to test multiple AI requests, audio transcription, and video exports.
  • Alternative: Reviewers may also use "Sign in with Google" if preferred.

2. Quick 2-Minute Review Path

1Launch & Authenticate

Open Trimm and tap Sign in with Apple. You will arrive immediately at the project creation screen.

2Select a Video Clip

Tap "Select clips to edit". Grant standard Photos library access and select any short sample video (such as a 10–30 second talking-head or camera roll clip). Trimm will import the footage locally and prepare the interactive timeline.

3Try Conversational AI Edits

In the chat input bar at the bottom, type any natural language editing command. Here are sample prompts to test:

"Cut out all awkward pauses and silences" "Add clean subtitles to the video" "Apply a warm vintage film color grading" "Make the video tighter"

Trimm's AI planner processes the request and returns an instant interactive preview proposal.

4Preview & Apply

Review the proposal card. You can scrub the on-device timeline to see the exact cuts or overlays in real time. Tap Accept to commit the edit to your timeline, or Reject to dismiss it.

5Export to Camera Roll

Tap Export Video. Trimm renders the video directly on your device via native Apple AVFoundation and saves the final file to your Camera Roll (0 credits deducted; exporting is completely free).

3. Privacy & On-Device Processing Architecture

Trimm is designed around a privacy-first, on-device rendering engine:

  • Raw Video Files: High-definition video footage never leaves the user's device. All video timeline compositing, color grading filters, and rendering are executed locally via Apple AVFoundation.
  • Cloud AI Processing: Only the audio track (for speech transcription via AssemblyAI) and the text chat instructions are transmitted to our secure cloud server for timeline planning. No video content is stored or used for model training.
