System explainer

One request. A grounded edit.

How a video becomes
“ready to publish.”

trimm does not upload everything and hope for a good answer. It first maps the footage locally, sends only the useful pieces for the request, then turns the agent’s understanding into edits you can preview.

You “Make this video publishable. Keep the pricing explanation.”

End-to-end flow

From chat to finished video

  1. 01
    L1

    Map locally

    The app reads duration, rotation, speech timing, scenes, technical signals and uncertain moments.

    Original footage stays on your device.
  2. 02
    ↗

    Select evidence

    It chooses broad story coverage, likely edit candidates and uncertain sections relevant to your request.

    Each selected range has a reason.
  3. 03
    AI

    Watch and understand

    Gemini 3.6 Flash watches those selected ranges and connects speech, visuals and surrounding context.

    It can request another precise range.
  4. 04
    ✓

    Propose, don’t mutate

    The agent returns typed operations with timestamps and supporting evidence—not executable commands.

    Stale or unsupported edits are rejected.
  5. 05
    ▶

    Preview the result

    The same timeline powers preview and export. Approving an edit updates the preview immediately.

    You can reject, revise or undo.
